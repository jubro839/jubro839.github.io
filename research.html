Research

Research Interests

  • Decision-focused learning for portfolio construction: learning estimator hyper-parameters (e.g. robust covariance thresholds) directly from realized portfolio risk while preserving the exact deployed estimator.
  • Large language models and agents in finance: news-driven risk review, auditing "blind" trading benchmarks, information design for multi-agent investment teams, and text-derived alpha signals.
  • Graph and time-series learning for spatio-temporal systems: graph neural networks and pattern matching for traffic-incident propagation; regime detection for market risk.

Working Papers (Under review)

Submitted to ICAIF 2026, the 7th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (Milan, Nov. 2026). One-page summaries are linked; full manuscripts are under double-blind review.

[W5] Lee, J. et al., DiffGerber: Decision-Focused Learning of the Gerber Threshold. First author.[summary]
Learns the Gerber covariance threshold from realized portfolio risk while every forward pass evaluates the exact hard statistic. On the top-100 US stocks, annual volatility falls from 14.04% to 13.76%, turnover from 0.87 to 0.76, and Sharpe rises from 0.44 to 0.54.
[W4] AURORA: Attention Universes for Risk Observation and Review Allocation. Second author.[summary]
Allocates a fixed daily review capacity between price-risk names and names selected by news, with source-linked LLM records. Over about 2M article, security and date records (2019 to 2025), replacing 20 to 25% of the list retains 93.5 to 93.8% of the tail-loss mass captured by a price-only queue.
[W3] CallRank: Isolating What Changed in Earnings-Call Q&A for Sector Ranking and Cost-Adjusted Alpha. Third author.[summary]
Represents each firm-month by the signed displacement of its Q&A embedding from the firm's own history. Monthly sector rank IC of .158; the Top-1 sector portfolio returns 42.1% annualized net of costs versus 24.0% for SPY.
[W2] AlphaLeak: What "Blind" LLM Trading Benchmarks Still See. Third author.[summary]
A utility and linkability audit of name-masked stock-ranking benchmarks: same-date news linkage recovers 99.8% of masked records and an LLM names the issuer on 71% of rows; theme/event abstraction cuts recovery to 3.9 to 5.1% without improving utility.
[W1] The Division of Research: Information Access and Independent-Book Capacity in LLM Investment Teams. Third author.[summary]
Introduces the Effective-Book Curve for LLM investment teams. Splitting 32 point-in-time fields across seats raises effective independent books at seven seats from 2.05 to 3.98, with 68% fewer prompt tokens and 52% lower model cost.
